### Research Interests
My research goal is to help robots and interactive agents learn more effectively from the everyday users with whom they interact and collaborate. I focus on how robots can use additional information already available in an interaction, like facial expressions or body language, and how robots can prompt users for more information, such as proactively reminding them to give feedback. Ultimately, I'm trying to make robots better at "reading the room" so they can be more helpful collaborators in our daily lives.
